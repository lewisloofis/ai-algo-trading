## Summary

1. We tested one idea on 2014-2021 data only: buy a stock the day after an index provider's forced selling of it is finished (a deletion) and hold it 5, 10 or 20 trading days, to see whether it rebounds by more than trading costs.
2. 15 test cells were fixed in advance; 6 had enough events (30+ events, 12+ reviews), 6 were too small and 3 had no data (all IDX: not transcribed yet).
3. Before costs, 1 of 6 tested cells beat simply holding the same stock (t >= 2); the strongest is pooled k=20 with an average edge of 457.0 bps (t 2.90). Some cells below the sample floor look strong (MSCI_ST k=10, MSCI_ST k=20) but are not scored: too few events or reviews.
4. After realistic trading costs and the other checks, 1 of 6 tested cells pass (the cost gate alone passes in 6 of 6); most common failing gates: G2 (5 cells), G7 (5 cells), G4 (3 cells).
5. Only the pooled cell passed: not a candidate, investigate which provider drives it. The holdout (2022 onward) has NOT been run or touched.
